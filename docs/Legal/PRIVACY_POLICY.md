# SuperSploit Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 14, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how the SuperSploit Development Team ("SuperSploit", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information related to the use of the SuperSploit Framework, particularly its Pro Tier features and associated license management system.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you activate or use the SuperSploit Pro Framework, our proprietary Security Engine (`supersploit_auth`) and license management system collect certain information ("License Data") to validate your license, prevent unauthorized use, and ensure the integrity of our software. This License Data includes:

*   **Hardware ID (HWID):** A unique identifier derived from your computer's hardware components.
*   **Software ID (SWID):** A unique identifier related to your software installation.
*   **External IP Address:** The public IP address of your network connection at the time of activation or license check.
*   **Geographic Region:** An approximate geographical location derived from your IP address.
*   **Timezone:** The timezone setting of your operating system.
*   **Activation Timestamps:** Records of when a license was activated or last validated.
*   **Telemetry Data:** Limited, non-personally identifiable operational data related to license checks and system integrity (e.g., successful validation, attempted tampering flags).

We do **not** collect personal data such as your name, email address, payment information (which is handled by third-party payment processors), or any content you create or process with the SuperSploit Framework.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

The License Data collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

*   **License Validation and Management:** To verify the authenticity and validity of your SuperSploit Pro license, ensuring compliance with the PRO_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md.
*   **Anti-Piracy and Security:** To prevent unauthorized use, detect and deter license key sharing, and protect our intellectual property from circumvention attempts. This includes cryptographically locking licenses to unique environmental fingerprints.
*   **Remote Policy Enforcement:** To facilitate the global revocation of leaked keys and dynamic remote hardware locking, as described in the `README.md`.
*   **Operational Monitoring and Support:** To monitor the health and activity of our license servers, diagnose activation issues, and provide technical support related to your license.
*   **Activation Telemetry:** For internal development and security teams to receive real-time alerts on license activations, including machine fingerprints and network metadata, via secure webhooks (e.g., Discord/Slack). This data is used for immediate operational awareness and does not constitute long-term storage on these third-party platforms.

## 3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

*   **Storage:** License Data is stored on secure, encrypted servers controlled by the SuperSploit Development Team.
*   **Security Measures:** We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption, access controls, and regular security audits, to protect your License Data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
*   **Retention:** License Data is retained for the duration of your active license and for a reasonable period thereafter as necessary for legal, audit, and anti-piracy purposes.

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your License Data to outside parties for marketing or any other commercial purposes.

Your License Data may be shared in the following limited circumstances:

*   **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
*   **Protection of Rights:** To protect the rights, property, or safety of SuperSploit, our users, or others.
*   **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our license management infrastructure, provided they agree to keep this information confidential and comply with data protection laws.

As mentioned, activation telemetry is sent via secure webhooks (e.g., Discord/Slack) for real-time alerts to our development team. This is for notification purposes and does not imply long-term storage or processing of your License Data by these third-party platforms beyond the immediate notification.

## 5. Your Rights

Given the nature of the data collected (primarily technical identifiers for license validation), direct user rights such as access, correction, or deletion of specific License Data points are limited as they are integral to the functioning of your license. However, if you have concerns about your License Data, please contact us using the information below.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website or within the SuperSploit Framework. Your continued use of the SuperSploit Pro Framework after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
